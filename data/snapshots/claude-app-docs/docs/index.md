## Create an account

1Go to [claude.ai (opens in a new tab)](https://claude.ai)2Select **Continue with Google** or enter your email address3Follow the prompts to complete registration

All plans have access to the [Connectors Directory](/docs/connectors/directory). Free plans can also add one [custom connector](/docs/connectors/custom/add-unlisted#add-a-connector-by-url). Compare [Claude plans and pricing (opens in a new tab)](https://claude.com/pricing).Claude for Government access is set up by your agency's administrators, so you don't need to create an account. See the [Claude for Government guide](/docs/government/overview).
